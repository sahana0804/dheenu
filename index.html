01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I don't just capture pictures. I capture how a moment feels.

I'm Dheenu, someone who finds joy in capturing the beauty of everyday life. Photography is my passion, and I love turning simple moments into beautiful memories that people can look back on and smile. Whether it's a candid smile, a breathtaking view, or a special moment, I believe every frame has a story worth sharing. With my iPhone 13 and DJI Osmo Pocket 3, it’s not about having the perfect shot — it’s about capturing a feeling. Working at OBLU SELECT Sangeli Maldives, surrounded by the beauty of island life.
